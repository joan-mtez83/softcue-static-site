What it does

Three things, done properly. Nothing else.

A recording screen that gets out of the way

No chrome. Controls fade after a couple of seconds and come back with a tap. Auto-scroll, adjustable speed and font size, a subtle read line, front or rear camera.

AI rewrite, built in

Pick a tone, a length and an audience. SoftCue rewrites your script so it reads like you talk, then shows the original and the rewrite side by side. Keep whichever you like.

Scripts that stay on your phone

Save as many scripts as you want. Search them, tag them, see word count and estimated read time. They live on your device, not on our servers.
